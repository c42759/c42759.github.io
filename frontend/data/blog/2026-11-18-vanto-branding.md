Some of the best creative exercises do not start in front of a moodboard or inside a design sprint. They start in the middle of a crowd, over a casual conversation where someone admits they are stuck.

During a visit to the **Festival do Pão de Ló** in Ovar, I ended up talking with a woman who crafts handmade clothing and everyday accessories. She spoke with genuine pride about the pieces she makes—the fabrics, the stitching, the small personal details she adds for every customer—but admitted she wanted to rebrand her project and had hit a wall. She had no name, no visual direction, and no starting point that felt truly hers.

When a maker puts their own hands into every garment, generic brand names fall flat. The identity needed to come from her own story.

So I looked at her family name: **Vaz Pinto**.

![VA'NTO Ivory Linen Shirt with Burgundy Label and VP Cuff Monogram](/blog/2026-11-18-vanto-branding/Ivory_linen_shirt_with_burgundy_VANTO_label-4.jpg "VA'NTO Ivory Linen Shirt with Burgundy Label and VP Cuff Monogram")

---

## 1. The Naming Concept: From *Vaz Pinto* to *VA’NTO*

Rather than inventing an abstract word disconnected from her roots, I pulled the two halves of her family name together into a single, unmistakable mark:

- **VA** from *Vaz*
- **NTO** from *Pinto*
- Joined by a sharp apostrophe that acts as a tailor's stitch between the two names: **VA’NTO**

**VA’NTO** is a handmade clothing and accessories brand concept rooted directly in the **Vaz Pinto** family name. It creates made-to-order garments and everyday essentials—from breathable linen shirts to fabric pouches, glasses sleeves, and heavy canvas tote bags—with personal touches such as embroidered initials, custom linings, and carefully chosen finishes.

The family name gives the brand its identity; the details give each piece a connection to the person it was made for.

> **Client-Facing Brand Statement**
> *"VA’NTO makes clothing and accessories by hand, with details chosen for the person who will wear or carry them. Named after the Vaz Pinto family, the brand brings a personal signature to everyday pieces."*

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Dual-Mark System

To translate that story into a physical brand world, I explored a visual system built around tactile contrast, heritage warmth, and quiet confidence.

### The Dual Signature: `VA’NTO` + `VP`
A handmade label needs two levels of presence: one that anchors the garment on the inside or carry piece, and a quieter mark that rewards a closer look.

1. **The Primary Wordmark (`VA’NTO`)**: Set in a bold, wide-stance geometric sans-serif where the wedge-shaped apostrophe sits tucked cleanly between the `A` and `N`. It reads with modern clarity whether woven into a neck label, embroidered in raised thread on coarse linen, or stamped onto a heavy canvas patch.
2. **The Heritage Monogram (`VP`)**: Set in a classic serif on the shirt cuff. It serves a double role: a subtle nod to *Vaz Pinto* and a live demonstration of the bespoke embroidered initials that clients can commission on their own made-to-order pieces.

### Color & Material Palette
Instead of sterile minimalism, the palette draws from traditional Portuguese textile warmth and workshop utility:

| Element | Direction | Role in the Brand System |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deep Burgundy / Wine** | Rich oxblood-burgundy linen & twill | Primary brand color; evokes warmth, craft heritage, and permanence |
| **Warm Ivory / Ecru** | Unbleached linen & heavy natural canvas | Counterbalances the burgundy with airy, sun-lit softness |
| **Botanical Jacquard** | Tonal damask & cream-on-burgundy florals | Hidden interior linings and textured small-goods fabrics |
| **Aged Brass & Mother-of-Pearl** | Brushed antique zippers, rivets & shell buttons | Tactile hardware that ages naturally with daily carry |

---

## 3. Exploring the Brand World: Piece by Piece

To see how the identity lives across real materials, I built out five visual studies spanning garments, everyday carry, and the maker's own workshop.

### The Signature Linen Shirt
At the heart of the garment line is the made-to-order linen shirt. Shown in the opening study in warm ivory linen over a deep burgundy backdrop, the collar carries the woven burgundy `VA’NTO` neck label in cream thread. Down on the turned cuff, beside a natural mother-of-pearl button, sits the burgundy `VP` embroidery—turning a classic wardrobe staple into a personal keepsake.

---

### The Atelier Zip Pouch: Hidden Details Inside
Handmade accessories shine when the inside receives as much care as the outside.

![Burgundy VA'NTO Atelier Zip Pouch with Floral Lining](/blog/2026-11-18-vanto-branding/Burgundy_VANTO_Atelier_Zip_Pouch-1.jpg "Burgundy VA'NTO Atelier Zip Pouch with Floral Lining")

Crafted from coarse burgundy linen with raised cream `VA’NTO` embroidery and an antique brass zipper, the pouch opens to reveal a custom cream-and-burgundy botanical floral lining. Resting beside a matching burgundy linen shirt cuff (`VP` embroidered in cream), it shows how the two-color palette flips effortlessly between inverse combinations.

---

### The Floral Jacquard Glasses Sleeve: Texture as Identity
Not every piece needs an oversized logo to feel recognizable.

![Burgundy Floral VA'NTO Glasses Sleeve](/blog/2026-11-18-vanto-branding/Burgundy_floral_VANTO_glasses_sleeve-2.jpg "Burgundy Floral VA'NTO Glasses Sleeve")

For smaller everyday accessories like this soft glasses sleeve, the fabric itself takes center stage—a tonal burgundy floral damask weave with clean perimeter stitching. The branding steps back to a discreet woven side tab, letting the texture of the jacquard and the accompanying ivory shirt cuff tell the story.

---

### The Everyday Canvas Tote: Built for Daily Carry
A brand rooted in everyday life needs a piece that leaves the house every morning.

![VA'NTO Heavy Canvas Tote Bag in Warm Light](/blog/2026-11-18-vanto-branding/VANTO_Canvas_Tote_in_Warm_Light-3.jpg "VA'NTO Heavy Canvas Tote Bag in Warm Light")

Structured in unbleached heavy cotton canvas with herringbone-weave straps, the tote puts the burgundy `VA’NTO` wordmark front and center on the exterior slip pocket. Paired with a burgundy linen overshirt draped across a mid-century wooden chair, it bridges workshop utility and relaxed daily style.

---

### The Maker’s Atelier Apron: Where Every Piece Begins
Finally, a handmade brand belongs to the person at the cutting table as much as the customer wearing the finished shirt.

![Burgundy Twill Atelier Apron with VA'NTO Patch](/blog/2026-11-18-vanto-branding/Burgundy_atelier_apron_with_VANTO-5.jpg "Burgundy Twill Atelier Apron with VA'NTO Patch")

Designed in heavy burgundy twill with contrast ecru double-stitching, antique brass strap rings, and riveted utility pockets, the atelier apron wears a stitched cream canvas patch across the waist. It ties the entire concept back to its origin: one maker, working by hand, putting her family signature into every stitch.

---

## Final Thoughts

What started as an unplanned conversation in the middle of Ovar's *Festival do Pão de Ló* became a reminder that the strongest brand identities rarely need to be invented from scratch. Often, the name, the story, and the soul of the project are already sitting right there in the maker's own heritage—waiting to be stitched together.
