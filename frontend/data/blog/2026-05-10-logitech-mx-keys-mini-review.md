# Logitech MX Keys Mini: 5 Years Later (Review)

After using the Logitech MX Keys Mini for over 5 years, it has become an absolute staple on my desk. Here is my comprehensive review of this minimal yet powerful keyboard.

## About My Unit

My specific unit is the **Logitech MX Keys Mini US International (ISO layout)**. It features a compact 75% design with an L-shaped Enter key. This layout specifically caters to users like me who need to type accents and special characters easily.

Despite its small footprint, it offers smart backlighting, a remarkably stable, low-profile typing experience, and multi-device pairing via Bluetooth or Logi Bolt.

![Logitech MX Keys Mini](/blog/2026-05-10-logitech-mx-keys-mini-review/mx-mini-keys.jpg "Logitech MX Keys Mini")

### Key Features & Points

- **Layout:** US International (ISO) — Includes the L-shaped, tall Enter key and acts as a "dead key" system for accents (e.g., `'` + `e` = `é`).
- **Form Factor:** 75% minimalist layout (no number pad). It is ideal for saving desk space and keeps your mouse closer for better ergonomics.
- **Build:** Sturdy metal frame with spherically-dished keys designed for quiet, tactile, and stable typing.
- **Smart Features:** Proximity sensors activate the white backlighting automatically as your hands approach the keyboard.
- **Connectivity:** Connects to up to 3 devices simultaneously via Bluetooth Low Energy or the Logi Bolt USB Receiver (sold separately).
- **Battery Life:** USB-C rechargeable. Logitech claims it lasts up to 10 days with the backlight on, or up to several months with backlighting off. **In my case**, I usually get around **2 months of battery life**, switching actively between 3 connected devices all day.
- **Customization:** Fully functional with the Logitech Options+ software, which allows you to remap the F-keys (such as Dictation, Emoji, and Mute).

## The Verdict

The MX Keys Mini is an excellent tool for anyone who types heavily, switches between multiple machines (like a work laptop, personal PC, and tablet), and values a clean, minimalist desk setup. After half a decade of use, it still holds up as one of the best wireless keyboards on the market.
