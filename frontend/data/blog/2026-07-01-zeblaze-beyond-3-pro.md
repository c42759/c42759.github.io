If you are looking for an affordable yet stylish smartwatch, the **Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro** might have caught your eye. After spending some time with it, here are my thoughts on this budget-friendly wearable.

### Design and Display

Right out of the box, the first thing I noticed is that this is a **super light smartwatch**. You barely feel it on your wrist, making it comfortable to wear all day and night. The overall aesthetic is very familiar - it features a square design that is strikingly similar to an Apple Watch. 

![Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro Hero](/blog/2026-07-01-zeblaze-beyond-3-pro/hero.png "Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro")

The standout hardware feature is definitely the display. The size of the screen and the vibrant OLED panel are amazing for the readability of notifications. Everything looks crisp, bright, and easy to read even at a quick glance.

![Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro Closeup](/blog/2026-07-01-zeblaze-beyond-3-pro/closeup.png "Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro")

### Battery Life

Battery endurance is one of its strongest suits. In my testing, the battery was capable of handling an impressive **14 days on a single charge** under typical use. When pushing it harder - specifically 6 days with 3 training sessions a week while actively tracking my vitals - it still held up remarkably well. You won't have to worry about charging this every night.

![Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro Accessories](/blog/2026-07-01-zeblaze-beyond-3-pro/accessories.png "Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro")

### Software and Features

When paired with the recommended Android app, you get access to a **huge list of watch faces**, allowing you to customize the look to match your style or mood effortlessly.

![Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro Watchfaces](/blog/2026-07-01-zeblaze-beyond-3-pro/watchfaces.png "Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro")

The watch is also very good for controlling music directly from your wrist. However, my experience with the calling features was mixed. The watch allows you to make and receive Bluetooth calls. If you're into that, it's there - but it's a feature I personally do not like, though it might be useful for someone else. 

A significant bug I encountered was with the "ignore call" function. It simply **never worked**, neither when using the smartwatch to decline it nor when trying to ignore a call on the smartphone while connected. This is something to keep in mind if you rely on call management from your wrist.

### The Verdict

At a really good price, usually **under 40 euros**, the Zeblaze Beyond 3 Pro offers a lot of value. You get a beautiful OLED screen, incredible battery life, and a lightweight Apple Watch-esque design for a fraction of the cost. Just be prepared for a few software quirks regarding call management.
